The adoption process

They say the animal chooses you, not you choos the animal. Come in today and find your next 4 legged friend. All of your dogs and cats are current of shots with a clean bill of health and spayed or neutered. The paperwork is simple and our adoption fees are rated fair to the breed, starting at $25 dollars.
